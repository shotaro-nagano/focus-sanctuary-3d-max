# ART PLAN — CHRONO CHRYSALIS · CLEFT VAULT

Selected concept (judge panel 2/3, totals 249 vs 246 vs 219) with grafts from the runner-up MERCURY SEAM and ESCAPEMENT CHRYSALIS.

Parameter names in this plan map 1:1 onto `SceneParams` in `src/shared/params.ts` (shellOpen, shellTwist, shellBreath, doorSwing, keelSplay, hubLift, ribbonTwist, ribbonSpread, ribbonReveal, coreGlow, coreScale, accentHue, orbitTilt, orbitReveal, progress, seamLight, shockwave, timeScale, attract, flowSpeed, liquidAmp, rimIntensity, envRotation, lightSlide, particleAlpha, floorPresence, bloom*, desaturate, vignette, aberration, grain, exposure, cam*, pointerWeight, root*). Where the plan says 'orbitTilt' etc. use exactly those fields.

## Tagline
A five-petal chrome vault, seams spiralled shut, with one diagonal bite torn out of it so you can see the hexagonal crystal and glass sashes it keeps inside. Time winds the seams tighter; completion springs them open.

## Silhouette
FRONT (desktop, camera yaw -18 deg, pitch +8): a tall bottom-heavy ovoid, 3.2 units tall, 2.2 wide, bulging at 44% of its height (not an egg on end, not a capsule; more like a seed pod milled from one billet). Five spiral seam lines (helical shear 0.45 rad over the height) cut the mass into unequal petals (96/78/60/84/42 deg). Upper-left, one petal is missing its top: a diagonal cleft whose thick 0.12-unit lip reads as machined metal, and through it you see three brushed-chrome keel ribs and the six-sided glass crystal core glowing citron. Right side, an equatorial slit (0.07 units tall) runs 40% of the way around, leaking a line of interior light. Small chrome polar hubs (r 0.22) cap top and bottom. Three incomplete blade-arcs (broken halo) cross the body: the biggest passes in front bottom-left and behind top-right. 3/4 VIEW: the petals show their thickness at every seam and at the cleft lip; the narrow 42-deg petal (the "door") is visibly a separate slab; the seams are clearly helical, not straight meridians; the tilted arcs separate from each other in depth. SIDE: the asymmetric bulge below centre, the protruding top hub, the door petal ajar, one arc seen edge-on as a hairline, the other as a broken ellipse. It is recognizable from any angle by four things: the diagonal bite, the spiral seams, the hexagonal crystal seen through the openings, and the broken halo. None of these is a stock primitive silhouette.

## Geometry plan (procedural, built once)
UNITS: sculpture height 3.2, centred at origin, y up. All geometry built once at startup as BufferGeometry via helper functions; nothing re-allocated per frame.

1) LATHE PROFILE (shared by shell, keel, glass band). v in [0,1] bottom->top. y(v) = -1.6 + 3.2 v. r(v) = 1.10 * pow(sin(pi * pow(v, 0.85)), 0.70). The pow(v,0.85) inside the sine moves the bulge to v = 0.443 (below centre, monolithic/bottom-heavy); the 0.70 exponent flattens the belly into a broad shoulder instead of a round egg. Thickness t(v) = 0.07 + 0.09 * pow(1 - abs(2v - 1), 1.4) + delta_i, delta_i per petal in {+0.02, -0.01, +0.03, 0, -0.02} (thick at the equator, thin at the poles, every petal slightly different).

2) OUTER SHELL = 8 thick panels from 5 petals. Seam-sheared angle theta' = theta + 0.45 * (v - 0.5) (helical seams). Petal spans (deg, 4-deg seam gaps): P0 [0,96), P1 [100,178), P2 [182,242), P3 [246,330), P4 [334,376). Cuts: P0 is split by the equatorial slit v in [0.56,0.63] into panels 0a (v 0.03..0.56) and 0b (0.63..0.97). P1 has the same slit but only over theta 100..139, giving 3 panels (lower full-span 0.03..0.56; upper-left 100..139 at 0.63..0.97; upper-right 139..178 at 0.56..0.97). P2 has the diagonal cleft: its top edge is v_top(theta) = 0.52 + 0.30 * (theta - 182)/60, so an upper triangle is missing. P3 and P4 are full (0.03..0.97). Panel builder buildPanel({th0, th1, v0, v1(theta), t(v)}) emits six independently-gridded faces so edges stay hard (no shared normals across creases): outer surface P(theta,v,0), inner surface P(theta,v,1) with P(theta,v,w) = ((r(v) - w t(v)) cos theta', y(v), (r(v) - w t(v)) sin theta'), two seam-side caps (w 0..1 x v), top and bottom caps (theta x w). Grid: 40 x 110 for outer and inner (4400 verts each), 6 x 110 for side caps, 40 x 6 for end caps: ~10.6k verts per panel, ~74k verts / ~140k tris for the shell. Merged with BufferGeometryUtils.mergeGeometries into two geometries per panel: "skin" (outer+inner) and "caps" (4 caps) because caps carry the seam-light shader. Each face carries attribute aV (the v of the vertex) used by the progress seam light. Vertex-shader twist (uniform uTwist, applied in onBeforeCompile begin_vertex): rotate position about Y by uTwist * (position.y / 1.6); rotate the normal by the same angle. This animates the spiral of the seams continuously without rebuilding geometry.

3) HINGED OPENING (Object3D hierarchy, no vertex work). sculptureRoot -> petalPivot_i -> [skinMesh, capsMesh]. Each pivot sits at the petal's bottom-mid: (0.35 cos thm, -1.5, 0.35 sin thm), thm = petal mid-angle. Open pose per frame: translate along (cos thm, 0, sin thm) by 0.55 * shellOpen * w_i, then rotate about the tangent axis (-sin thm, 0, cos thm) by 28 deg * shellOpen * w_i, with per-petal weights w = [1.0, 0.55, 0.8, 0.35, 0.7] so it never opens symmetrically. P4 (the 42-deg door) has a second, vertical hinge at its edge theta = 334, swinging 60 deg * clamp((shellOpen - 0.5) * 2, 0, 1) and 90 deg during M10. Polar hubs (LatheGeometry, 24 x 12 profile, r 0.22, h 0.18, hex-chamfered) stay fixed to sculptureRoot so opening reads as a mechanism separating from a chassis.

4) DOUBLE STRUCTURE / KEEL (the second wall). Three meridian ribs at 0.78 r(v), v 0.06..0.94, at angles 20, 150, 262 deg (deliberately not equal), rectangular cross-section 0.07 x 0.12, swept along the profile with a generic sweepBox(curve, w, h, segs) helper (120 segments x 8 verts each; ~2.9k verts). One latitude ring at v = 0.44 (same sweep on a circle, 300-deg arc leaving a gap facing the cleft). The ribs splay 12 deg outward in M10 (rotation about the bottom hub). Brushed chrome, darker.

5) GLASS RIBBONS (3). Helical sash curve C_k(s) = (a(s) cos phi(s), y(s), a(s) sin phi(s)), s in [0,1]: y(s) = -1.1 + 2.2 s, phi(s) = phi0_k + 2 pi * (0.85 + ribbonTwist) * s, a(s) = ribbonSpread * (0.55 + 0.18 sin(pi s)); phi0_k = 0, 2.2, 4.1 rad (unequal). Cross-section: flat band, width w(s) = 0.22 * smoothstep(0, 0.12, s) * smoothstep(1, 0.88, s) (tapers to points), thickness 0.035, band face normal radial. Built as sweepBox with 240 segments x 8 verts = 1920 verts per ribbon (5.8k total). Because it is this small, morphing is done on the CPU: while ribbonTwist/ribbonSpread are tweening, positions are recomputed analytically into the existing BufferAttribute (needsUpdate = true) and normals recomputed; at ~6k verts this is <0.3 ms. (Shader-side reconstruction from (s, side, layer) attributes is the optimization path if needed.) M03 surface life: vertex shader adds 0.012 * sin(9 s + 1.7 t) along the normal.

6) CORE. Not a sphere: a six-sided spindle. LatheGeometry with radialSegments = 6 (literally a hexagonal prism crystal) over profile rc(v) = 0.19 * pow(1 - pow(abs(2v - 1), 2.2), 0.6), height 0.9, 24 profile points (~170 verts), glass. Inside it, at 0.55 scale, a second identical lathe as the emissive filament plus three thin emissive rings (TorusGeometry r 0.09, tube 0.006, 48 x 8) at v 0.3/0.5/0.7 that counter-rotate. Core group bobs on its own 5.3 s period.

7) ORBIT FRAGMENTS (3 blade arcs, rectangular section 0.05 x 0.16, sweepBox on circle arcs, 240 seg x 8 = 1.9k verts each). Arc A: radius 1.9, span 210 deg, tilt 24 deg about X. Arc B: radius 2.35, span 130 deg, tilted 65 deg about the axis (1,0,0.6), with a 0.5-unit-long counterweight block (BoxGeometry 0.5 x 0.12 x 0.2) at one end. Arc C: radius 1.55, span 300 deg with two 8-deg notches, attribute aT = 0..1 along the arc; this is the PROGRESS arc (fragment-shader clip at uProgress with a bloom-hot citron tip). Rotation periods 41 s / 67 s / 29 s on three different axes, phases 0 / 1.9 / 4.4 rad; orbitTilt blends each arc's rest orientation between a "scattered" set and an "aligned" gyroscope set (see modes).

8) PARTICLES. Three THREE.Points layers, one ShaderMaterial: near 400 (size 18-40 px, soft disc, low alpha = fake DOF), mid 2500 (4-7 px), far 6000 (1.5-2.5 px, dim). Positions are base + flow(t) evaluated in the vertex shader: a slow vortex about Y (period 20 s) plus a sin/cos drift and an optional attractor term toward the core (uAttract) used in M10. Time comes from a uniform uSceneTime accumulated on the CPU as sceneTime += dt * timeScale, so pausing freezes phase and resuming continues it.

9) FLOOR. Plane 30 x 30 at y = -2.4. High preset: examples/jsm/objects/Reflector at 512 x 512 with a custom fragment (blurred sample mixed 18% into a near-black surface, fading with distance via a radial mask) plus a soft contact-shadow blob (ShaderMaterial, radial gradient whose radius tracks float height). Medium preset: contact shadow only.

Totals: ~100k verts, ~25 draw calls (8 skins, 8 caps, 3 ribs + ring, 3 ribbons, crystal + filament + 3 rings, 3 arcs + block, 2 hubs, 3 point clouds, floor + shadow).

## Material & light plan
ENVIRONMENT (the thing that makes chrome look like chrome): a procedural "studio" scene rendered once through PMREMGenerator.fromScene(envScene, 0.02, 0.1, 200) and set as scene.environment. Contents: an inverted 50-unit sphere with a vertical-gradient ShaderMaterial (top #1A1C22, horizon #06070A, bottom #0B0C10 with a faint 0.02-grey floor-bounce band so downward-facing chrome is dark grey, not void). Five light shapes with MeshBasicMaterial colours above 1.0 (PMREM keeps HDR in half-float): L1 "softbox" plane 12 x 1.2 at (0, 9, -4) facing down, colour (7, 7, 7.2); L2 vertical strip 1.0 x 14 at (-10, 0, 2), colour (6.5, 6.6, 7); L3 curved strip = partial torus (R 12, tube 0.5, arc 100 deg) at (9, 3, -6), colour (5, 5, 5.4); L4 low citron strip 6 x 0.4 at (2, -6, 5), colour (1.6, 2.4, 0.4); L5 small pink-violet patch 1 x 1 at (7, -2, 8), colour (0.9, 0.6, 0.8). Roughly 20-25% of the sphere is bright, so any curved chrome surface sees at least one long white bar; the bars are long and thin, which is what produces the sliding white "liquid" highlights on the lathe curvature. scene.environmentRotation (Euler, available since r162) is nudged +-6 deg by the pointer and drifts 0.5 deg/s so reflections slide across the body even when the sculpture is still (M03/M04).

CHROME: MeshPhysicalMaterial { color #DCE0E6, metalness 1.0, roughness 0.05 (petal skins), envMapIntensity 1.3 } with a procedural 256 x 256 DataTexture roughnessMap (low-frequency value noise remapped to 0.03..0.12) so the mirror has faint waviness rather than a uniform mirror. Petal caps: roughness 0.14. Hubs 0.10. Arcs 0.18. Keel: roughness 0.28, anisotropy 0.6 with anisotropyRotation along the rib (brushed). Anti-black insurance in two more layers: (1) onBeforeCompile inserts after <emissivemap_fragment>: float fr = pow(1.0 - saturate(dot(normalize(vViewPosition), normal)), 3.0); totalEmissiveRadiance += uRim.rgb * fr * uRim.a; with uRim platinum (#C9CDD4) at 0.35, so every silhouette edge carries a thin light rim against black regardless of env; (2) a DirectionalLight key (white, 1.2, upper-left) and a citron rim DirectionalLight (0.6, behind-right) which give tiny specular pins on chrome and do the real lighting for glass and the brushed keel. A debug key toggles a flat-grey material to verify env coverage quickly.

GLASS (ribbons, crystal): MeshPhysicalMaterial { color #F4F8FF, metalness 0, roughness 0.05, transmission 1.0, thickness 0.35 (ribbons) / 0.5 (crystal), ior 1.55 / 1.75, attenuationColor #DDE9FF, attenuationDistance 1.2, dispersion 0.35 (r168+), iridescence 0.6, iridescenceIOR 1.3, iridescenceThicknessRange [120, 400], envMapIntensity 1.6, side DoubleSide, depthWrite true }. Dispersion gives the spectral fringes only on thick edges; iridescence gives the pale violet/pink sheen at grazing angles. Cost: three.js renders the opaque scene to the transmission buffer once per frame; renderer.transmissionResolutionScale = 1.0 desktop, 0.5 mobile. Known limit: transmissive objects do not refract each other (each only sees the opaque buffer), so the crystal is placed centrally and the ribbons wrap outside it; where a ribbon crosses in front of the crystal you see the crystal's own render, not a refracted one. Overlap flicker is handled by renderOrder sorted by distance each frame and a hard depthWrite. CHEAP FALLBACK (medium preset or if transmission tanks fps): transmission 0, transparent true, opacity 0.42, blending Normal, envMapIntensity 2.2, iridescence kept, plus a fresnel emissive edge (uRim ice #BFE9FF at 0.5) via the same injection. Reads as glass with bright edges and reflections; you lose background distortion, which the README will state.

CORE EMISSIVE: filament + rings use a ShaderMaterial outputting uAccent * uCoreGlow * (0.8 + 0.2 sin(pulse)) with uCoreGlow 0..3, so values above 1.0 are the only thing that reliably crosses the bloom threshold. The crystal shell itself is glass with emissive #1a2000 * 0.4 so it looks lit from within. A PointLight (citron, intensity 0.8 * coreGlow, distance 3) sits at the core and touches the keel, caps and ribbons.

POST: EffectComposer on a HalfFloat target: RenderPass -> UnrealBloomPass(threshold 1.0, strength 0.55, radius 0.6) -> final ShaderPass (vignette 0.35, radial chromatic aberration 0.0025 at the edges only, film grain 0.03, paused-mode desaturation uniform). Threshold 1.0 means only the core, the progress tip and the hottest bar reflections bloom; chrome edges and DOM type never wash out. ACESFilmic tone mapping, exposure 1.05, outputColorSpace sRGB. Bloom is 0.35 while paused, 0.9 for 0.8 s in M10.

### Grafted from MERCURY SEAM (material)
ENVIRONMENT (the reason chrome will not go black): a procedural env scene rendered once through PMREMGenerator.fromScene(envScene, 0.04). Contents: (a) a dome: an inverted sphere with a vertical gradient shader, luminance 0.18 at the zenith -> 0.06 at the horizon -> 0.02 at the nadir, tinted #7C8290 top / #1A1C22 bottom. This guarantees a non-black base reflection everywhere. (b) a large softbox quad 4x1 units above-left, MeshBasicMaterial white, intensity 6 (broad soft highlight). (c) a long thin strip 6 x 0.12 behind-right of the camera, intensity 14, warm-white #FFF6EA - this is the signature white band that slides across curvature. (d) a curved vertical strip (cylinder arc, 90 deg, 0.15 wide) camera-left, cool white #E6F0FF, intensity 9. (e) a low citron strip 3 x 0.08 behind the sculpture, intensity 2.5, #D9FF62 - undersides pick up a faint accent. The env is neutral and mode-independent; mode color comes from lights and an envTint uniform (below), so no PMREM regeneration at runtime. RectAreaLightUniformsLib.init() + one RectAreaLight 4 x 0.3, intensity 8, white, above-right, gives an additional real elongated highlight that survives whatever the env sampling does. Extra lights: DirectionalLight rim from back-right (cool, 2.5), citron PointLight at the core (intensity 0..40 driven by coreGlow, distance 3, decay 2) which lights the inner sheet of the far petal - the metal interior glows from the core, the key 'light between' read.

CHROME: MeshPhysicalMaterial with onBeforeCompile injecting the superformula/noise vertex code (replacing <begin_vertex>, <beginnormal_vertex>) and a fragment injection that (1) modulates roughness per fragment between 0.05 and 0.16 with a large-scale noise varying (polished vs faintly hazed zones - not uniform 0), (2) multiplies the indirect specular by mix(1, envTint, 1-NdotV*NdotV) so shadowed/glancing regions carry the mode color while frontal reflections stay platinum. Params: color #E6E9EE, metalness 1.0, roughness 0.08, clearcoat 0.6, clearcoatRoughness 0.03 (second sharp highlight = liquid), envMapIntensity 1.35, specularIntensity 1.0, side FrontSide (inner sheet exists as real geometry, so no DoubleSide needed). Rim strips get roughness 0.04 so the cut lips flare.

GLASS (desktop): MeshPhysicalMaterial transmission 1.0, thickness 0.35, ior 1.7, dispersion 0.25 (r185 supports it), roughness 0.05, iridescence 0.35, iridescenceIOR 1.3, iridescenceThicknessRange [100,400] (spectral only near grazing edges, matching the palette rule), attenuationColor #CFE8FF, attenuationDistance 1.2, envMapIntensity 1.2, FrontSide, renderOrder after the shell; renderer.transmissionResolutionScale 1.0 desktop / 0.5 mid. Because the ribbons are real thin boxes, transmission thickness produces genuine thickness darkening at the edges. GLASS fallback (mobile/mid preset): custom ShaderMaterial: reflect = textureCube(env, R), refract per channel with ior 1.68/1.70/1.72 sampling the env, Fresnel-Schlick F0 0.06 mix, thin-film edge hue (hue = fract(0.35 + 1.8*(1-NdotV))) at 0.25 strength, plus an additive glow term from the core (distance-based) so the ribbon still looks lit from inside. It does not refract the actual scene, and this will be stated in the README.

RIBBON EDGE TUBES: ShaderMaterial, additive, color mix(citron, ice, coreHue) with a travelling band: intensity = smoothstep of fract(s - T*flowSpeed) with band length 0.18, and a 'fuse' term that lights the whole length up to s < progress (the burning-fuse progress display). Emissive values 2.5-4.0 so they cross the bloom threshold.

CORE: ShaderMaterial, emissive gradient citron (#D9FF62) at the center -> white at the fresnel edge, intensity coreGlow * 3, plus an inner noise mottling. Additive Points sparks 1.8.

BLOOM: UnrealBloomPass strength 0.55, radius 0.35, threshold 0.85, on a half-res buffer. Only core, edge bands, sparks and real strip highlights exceed 0.85, so shell mid-tones and the DOM text (outside WebGL entirely) never blow out. During M10 the threshold tweens to 0.6 for 1.2 s. Post chain: RenderPass -> Bloom -> a single custom shader pass doing ACES tonemap-lite, vignette 0.35, chromatic aberration 0.0025 (edges only, radial), grain 0.035, desaturation uniform for pause. Output: renderer.toneMapping NoToneMapping (done in the pass), SRGB output.

## Mode mapping (FORM_POSES / CAMERA_POSES already encode the numbers)

- **idle**: shellOpen 0.22 (seams visibly ajar, glass glimpsed through 4-deg gaps widened to ~0.12 units), shellTwist 0.15, shellBreath 0.6, ribbonTwist 1.0, ribbonSpread 0.9, coreGlow 0.8 with 6 s pulse, accentHue 0.3 (platinum-citron), coreScale 0.9, orbitTilt 0 (scattered arcs), timeScale 1, camera dolly 5.2 / yaw -18 / pitch +8, sculpture offset x +0.9 on desktop. Slow root yaw 90 s, keel counter-yaw 140 s, float bob 7 s / 11 s.
- **focus**: Tween 1.2 s (M06/M07): shellOpen 0.22 -> 0.06 (petals slide in and seal; only the diagonal cleft and equatorial slit stay open as windows), shellTwist -> 0.35 then driven by progress toward 0.95, ribbonTwist -> 1.6 (tight coil), ribbonSpread -> 0.7, coreGlow -> 2.4 sharp citron with 1.2 s pulse, accentHue 0, orbitTilt -> 1 (arcs snap into a near-horizontal gyroscope and spin 2.3x, period ~18 s), progress arc begins filling, camera dolly 4.4 / yaw +24 / pitch -4 (low angle, monolithic; top pole crops out of frame on purpose), timeScale 1, root yaw slows to 160 s so the vault reads as locked.
- **shortBreak**: Tween 1.4 s: shellOpen -> 0.55 (all petals slide out ~0.3 and tilt ~15 deg, door begins to swing), shellTwist -> 0, ribbonTwist -> 0.6, ribbonSpread -> 1.25 (sashes emerge through the seams), coreGlow -> 1.2, accentHue -> 1 (ice #BFE9FF), coreScale 1.0, orbitTilt -> 0.4, timeScale 0.8, camera dolly 5.6 / yaw -40 / pitch +14, bloom 0.5.
- **longBreak**: Tween 1.6 s: shellOpen -> 1.0 (petals out 0.55, tilted 28 deg, door swung 60 deg), shellTwist -> -0.2 (seams relax the other way), ribbonTwist -> 0.3 (near-straight hanging sashes), ribbonSpread -> 1.6, coreGlow -> 1.0 with 8 s pulse, accentHue -> 2 (warm #FFD2A8), coreScale 1.15, orbitTilt -> 0.2 with arc periods 1.6x slower, timeScale 0.6, float amplitude 1.5x, camera dolly 7.0 / yaw -65 / pitch +18 (wide, the whole opened mechanism in frame), root yaw 60 s (more visible rotation).
- **paused**: Form stays exactly at the current mode/progress pose. timeScale 1 -> 0 over 1.4 s (power3.out) so arcs, particles, ribbon drift, pulses and floats decelerate together and freeze with their phases intact (sceneTime stops accumulating). shellBreath -> 0, coreGlow x0.6, accentHue lerps 40% toward platinum, rim intensity 0.35 -> 0.55 (edges sharpen), bloom 0.55 -> 0.35, final-pass desaturation 0.25, camera dolly -6% and pitch +3 deg. The frozen frame is composed to work as a still: key bar reflection across the shoulder, cleft lit from within.
- **complete**: Runs the M10 timeline (see completeBeats) from the focus pose: converge (particles uAttract 1, ribbonSpread -> 0.45, progress arc collapses), release (shellTwist 0.95 -> -0.4, shellOpen -> 1.35, door 90 deg, keel splay 12 deg, hub lift 0.4, ribbonSpread -> 2.2, coreGlow 3.0 with expanding shockwave ring), then re-settle with elastic ease into the shortBreak or longBreak pose and its camera; the timer shows the next mode's duration in waiting state. Record increments happen in the timer module, never from the animation.

## Progress mapping
Focus progress p (0..1, computed from the real end time) is smoothed each frame toward its target with a 0.5 s time constant (never stepped per second), then drives four visible things. (1) SEAM LIGHT: every petal cap face carries attribute aV; the cap fragment shader adds uAccent * 1.8 * smoothstep(uSeamHeight + 0.015, uSeamHeight - 0.05, vV) with uSeamHeight = p, so a citron line climbs the spiral seams from the bottom pole to the top pole over the 25 minutes; at p = 1 the whole seam network is lit. (2) SHELL TWIST: shellTwist = lerp(0.35, 0.95, p): the seams visibly wind tighter as the session progresses (the chrysalis under tension), and M10 is literally this stored twist unwinding. (3) PROGRESS ARC: arc C's fragment shader discards aT > p and paints a 0.03-wide citron head at 2.5 intensity, so a broken halo grows around the vault with a bloom-hot tip. (4) CORE: coreScale = lerp(0.8, 1.15, p) and pulse period lerp(1.6 s, 1.0 s, p). Decorative rotation (root yaw, arc spin, ribbon drift, float) uses sceneTime and is independent of p, so the two are never confused; on REPLAY the intro re-runs and then the pose is re-applied from the live p.

## Intro (M01) beats
Camera path: CatmullRomCurve3 through 4 keys, eased with GSAP power3.inOut on a single 0..1 parameter; every other value is a GSAP timeline on the params object (label 'intro', killable and re-runnable for REPLAY without touching timer state).
0.00-0.50 s: MACRO. Camera at dolly 1.1, positioned inside the seam gap between petals 0 and 1 at v ~0.5, looking along the seam: frame is the polished cap lip with the L2 vertical bar reflected as a white sliver sliding across it (environmentRotation animates 12 deg in this half second), out-of-focus ribbon edge behind (near-particle layer at max size for false DOF). uRim at 2x so the edge draws itself against black. shellOpen 0 (fully sealed), coreGlow 0, arcs parked at 90 deg tilt outside frame.
0.50-1.50 s: REVEAL. Dolly 1.1 -> 5.2 along the curve while yaw sweeps +60 -> -18 and pitch 0 -> +8; shellOpen 0 -> 0.26 overshooting to 0.22 (back.out(1.4)) so the petals unseal with a mechanical settle; coreGlow 0 -> 0.8 ramping from 0.9 s so the interior lights up exactly as the cleft comes into view; ribbonSpread 0.6 -> 0.9. Float and root yaw fade in (timeScale 0 -> 1).
1.00-2.10 s: TYPE. 'FOCUS' (back layer, behind the canvas) rises from a clip-path mask bottom-up, letters staggered 45 ms with a 6 deg skew that resolves to 0; because it is behind the canvas the sculpture occludes part of it immediately, establishing depth. 'SANCTUARY' (front layer) slides in from the right at 1.4 s crossing the lower body, hairline italic serif.
1.80-3.00 s: MECHANISM. ## Risks acknowledged

- Transmission cost: 3 ribbons + crystal force an extra opaque-scene render per frame, on top of bloom; on integrated GPUs at DPR 2 this can drop below 60 fps. Mitigation: transmissionResolutionScale 0.5, DPR cap, and the non-transmissive glass fallback wired into the quality preset from day one.
- Transmissive objects do not refract each other in three.js; ribbons crossing in front of the crystal show it un-refracted and overlapping ribbons can pop in sort order. Mitigation: renderOrder by distance, depthWrite on, keep the crystal central, accept and document the limit.
- onBeforeCompile injections (twist, rim fresnel, seam light, arc clip) depend on the exact chunk names in r185; a wrong replace silently produces no effect. Mitigation: assert the replace changed the string at startup and log; pin three to one version.
- Vertex-shader twist rotates normals approximately (ignores the derivative term); at shellTwist near 1.0 rad the reflections on the equator may shear slightly. Acceptable visually; can add the analytic correction if it shows.
- Black chrome if the PMREM env intensity or the HDR light values are mis-scaled (e.g. materials clamped to LDR). Mitigation: flat-grey debug material toggle, verify PMREM target is HalfFloat, verify a white bar is visible on the shoulder before building anything else.
- Reflector floor is a second full scene render; keep it optional (high preset only, 512 px) and never on mobile.
- Compositing conflicts between intro/M10 timelines, mode tweens, progress, pointer and drag: solved only if every parameter is written by a single applyPose() from a layered target object and GSAP uses overwrite:'auto' with killTweensOf on mode change; skipping this produces the classic stuck/vanishing sculpture on rapid clicks.
- CPU ribbon regeneration while tweening is cheap (~6k verts) but must recompute normals for the glass to look right; forgetting normals gives flat black bands.
- Feasibility for a single coder in a few hours is tight: the sculpture, env and materials alone are 2-3 hours; all 12 motions plus timer logic and QA are a full day. The plan is ordered so a stripped version (shell + keel + core + arcs, non-transmissive glass, intro + mode tweens) is still a coherent piece if time runs out.
